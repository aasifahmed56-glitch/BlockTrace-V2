BlockTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

BlockTrace is an academic digital-forensics project (Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education) for explainable blockchain crime investigation.

What we process

  • Public blockchain data: wallet addresses you enter are looked up against public blockchain data and a dated snapshot of the OFAC SDN sanctions list.
  • Forensic reports: PDF reports you upload for verification are processed in memory to compute hashes and are not stored.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Third-party services

The app uses Etherscan (on-chain data), Neo4j Aura (graph storage), Pinata/IPFS (report publishing) and the Ethereum Sepolia test network. Data written to IPFS or a blockchain is public and cannot be deleted.
